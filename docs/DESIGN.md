## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, intellectual, and strategic consultancy persona. Built for executive leadership, communications directors, and enterprise decision-makers, it trades conventional digital marketing tropes for the gravity and precision of modern high-end editorial publishing.

### Key Philosophy & Movement
- **Movement:** Crisp Neo-Grotesque Corporate Consultancy. A calculated hybrid of Swiss graphic rigor, high-contrast monochrome tones, and generous structural whitespace.
- **Tone:** Strategic, deliberate, calm, and intellectually credible. It avoids marketing hyperbole, aggressive bright gradients, and decorative UI clutter in favor of documentary-level clarity.
- **Core Principles:**
  - **No Decorative Icons:** Elimination of generic iconography and arbitrary status badges. Visual hierarchy relies strictly on proportion, typographical weight, layout rules, and hairline dividers.
  - **Structured Geometry:** Content organizes into strict modular frames and micro-bordered registers, evoking analytical frameworks, financial sheets, and prestige quarterly journals.
  - **Real Narrative Media:** Photography adheres to documentary realism—observational captures of teams, work environments, and architectural space—integrated with restrained hairline borders.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly disciplined around slate, charcoal, and pure neutrals. By removing saturated brand chromatic accents, the system anchors all cognitive attention onto messaging, layout relationships, and structural frameworks.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate/Ink. Used for dominant headings, primary buttons, high-priority borders, and key framing lines. Communicates absolute authority and precision.
- **Secondary (`#334155`):** Slate-700. Reserved for long-form narrative body copy, secondary button strokes, and section sub-headings.
- **Tertiary & Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool Slate-500. Applied to editorial kickers, metadata, index numbers, tabular captions, and tertiary micro-copy.
- **Surfaces & Borders:**
  - `surface-canvas` (`#FFFFFF`): Primary backdrop for hero sections, analytical frameworks, and active viewports.
  - `surface-subtle` (`#FAFAFA`): Controlled alternation for structural cards, contrast comparison panels, and secondary content blocks.
  - `border-hairline` (`#E2E8F0`): The primary organizational spine. Separates rows, metadata blocks, and funnel stages without optical heaviness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Manrope** for authoritative structural titling with **Inter** for neutral, utilitarian body and data delivery.

### Guidelines
- **Manrope:** Chosen for its geometric stability, sharp modern terminals, and balanced presence. Headlines must maintain tightened tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke deliberate architectural confidence rather than marketing flair.
- **Inter:** Chosen for neutral legibility. Operates unobtrusively in the background to sustain editorial readability over dense business copy and process descriptions.
- **Label Caps:** Formatted in uppercase with letter spacing (`0.08em`). Used strictly for eyebrows, category kickers, step indexes, and section anchors (e.g., `01 / CAPABILITY`, `STORY FUNNEL`).
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Never mix weights arbitrarily. Titles stay at `600` (SemiBold) or drop directly to body copy at `400` (Regular) for maximum contrast between statement and explanation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a 12-column grid anchored inside a maximum container width of `1280px`. Generous vertical breathing room establishes an intellectual tempo reminiscent of broadsheet journalism.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem), `margin-desktop` (5rem). Maximum canvas width capped at `1280px` with automated center alignment.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin-tablet` (3rem). Side-by-side modules convert to 2-column or stacked registers.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (1rem), `margin` (1.5rem). Horizontal process funnels transition into vertical chronological track systems.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Section Isolation:** Large vertical intervals (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolate concepts, ensuring readers digest one strategic proposition before encountering the next.
- **Grid Hairline Partitioning:** Containers and grid cells often share borders directly (`0.5px` to `1px` overlap) creating clean, co-planar editorial tables rather than floating, disconnected bubbles.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely avoids drop shadows, blurred ambient blooms, and artificial skeuomorphic layering. Spatial relationship is established through co-planar surface contrast and architectural lines.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA` representing the base reading plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Inset):** Panels, card registers, and comparison columns demarcated purely by `#E2E8F0` hairline borders.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus):** High-contrast inversion. Interactive highlights or prominent banners shift to `#0F172A` background with crisp white text.
- **Dividers & Traversal:** Horizontal and vertical lines using `#E2E8F0` (0.5px to 1px thickness) define grid registers. The visual metaphor for flow (such as the Story Funnel progression) relies on thin linear axis guides rather than drop-shadow card lifts.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 1`, shapes remain strictly disciplined and semi-sharp. 

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components:** Interactive elements such as buttons, form fields, and tags utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii. This subtle softening removes digital harshness while preserving geometric authority.
- **Containers & Panels:** Cards, content panels, and comparison containers use `0.25rem` (4px) or completely square `0px` outer edge alignments when integrated directly into the hairline structural grid.
- **No Pill Radii:** Rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are prohibited. They suggest consumer gamification or social media idioms, which contradicts the executive consultancy narrative.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Button:** Deep Slate fill (`#0F172A`), white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 500`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`, `rounded: 0.25rem`. Transitions on hover to subtle slate tone (`#1E293B`) without displacement or lift.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, crisp 1px outline (`#E2E8F0`), primary text (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions to hairline border `#0F172A` and `#FAFAFA` background.
- **Text Link CTA:** Editorial inline link accompanied by an em-dash or arrow character (`→`). Underline is static, 1px thick, positioned at `text-underline-offset: 4px`, rendering in `#0F172A`.

### The Linear Funnel Flow (Visual Metaphor)
- **Structure:** An editorial progression bar dividing stages horizontally across the grid: `Expertise → Story → Content → Attention → Engagement → Trust → Action → Sale`.
- **Styling:** Connected via a hairline border (`#E2E8F0`). Each stage sits inside an equal-width cell containing a `numeric-index` (e.g., `01`, `02`) in `#64748B`, followed by the stage name in `headline-md` or `label-md` (`#0F172A`). On mobile, reflows into a continuous vertical index list.

### Cards & Capability Columns
- **Editorial Card:** Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA` container enclosed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Padding `2rem`. Header includes a small uppercase tracking label (`label-caps`) indicating category or capability, followed by a tight `headline-md`, a divider, and explanatory body prose.
- **Model Comparison Table:** Two parallel columns comparing "Traditional Model" versus "Story Funnel Model". The traditional column carries muted typography (`#64748B`) and subtle borders; the consultancy column carries high-contrast text (`#0F172A`) and structured numbering to validate efficiency.

### Industry & Client Breadth Matrix
- **Text Grid:** Avoids decorative or stock icons. Renders target sectors (e.g., "Financial institutions", "Technology companies", "Professional services") as a structured 3- or 4-column typographic register separated by vertical and horizontal hairline borders. Each cell uses `body-md` with `font-weight: 500`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Input Fields:** Unfilled or white background, bordered by 1px `#E2E8F0`. Focused state switches border to `#0F172A` with zero glow/ring effect. Placeholder text is `#64748B`. Labels use `label-md` positioned directly above the input with a `0.375rem` gap.

### Navigation & Header
- **Top Bar:** Minimal, border-bottom 1px `#E2E8F0`. Contains text wordmark `STORY FUNNEL` in `Manrope` 600 tracking `0.02em`, flanked by text-only links in `label-md` (`#334155`), ending with a single compact Primary Button. Zero decorative icon menus.
