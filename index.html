02 // THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Your organisation already has the stories.

The challenge isn't always finding something to say. It's knowing: What should we say? How should we say it? Who should say it? Where should we say it? And how does it lead directly to business?

The Common Trap

Many organisations invest in social media managers, content creators, agencies, paid advertising and beautiful photography and video. Yet their social pages can still become a sterile stream of:

⚠️ The Generic Loop: Products → Promotions → Events → Photos → “Contact us” → Buy now
The Broken Result

They may gain passive follower counts, but generate little meaningful conversation, zero authentic trust, and zero real movement toward purchase decisions.

The Story Funnel Opportunity

The strategic knowledge that could make your brand magnetic is already sitting inside your organisation. Story Funnel unlocks it and trains your team to monetize it.

04 // THREE CORE PILLARS

Three capabilities. One system.

Pillar 01 📖

STORYTELLING

Turn internal expertise into compelling stories.

We identify the knowledge, people, experiences and ideas inside your organisation that can become powerful content. We teach teams story development, story structures, hooks, editorial thinking, brand storytelling, expert-led content, video storytelling and human-centred storytelling.

Core Competencies:

Hook Architecture · Narrative Arcs · Video Presence · Editorial Standards

Pillar 02 📱

SOCIAL MEDIA

Train teams to understand platforms, audiences, formats, analytics, and behaviour.

We help your team understand how people consume content today. Training covers platform behaviour, audience psychology, content formats, short-form video, social hooks, retention, engagement, content planning, analytics, lead generation, funnel, CTA, personal/team visibility and platform-specific storytelling.

Core Competencies:

Short-form Video · Algorithm Psychology · Audience Retention · Personal Branding

Pillar 03 📈

GROWTH & SALES

Connect content to engagement, leads, conversion and revenue.

Content should not simply generate views. We help organisations connect their content to the business through calls to action, customer journeys, lead generation, conversion, sales content, content funnels, audience nurturing, measurement and optimisation.

Core Competencies:

Conversion CTAs · Inbound Pipeline · Audience Nurturing · Closed Deal Attribution
