Many organisations invest in social media managers, content creators, agencies, paid advertising and beautiful photography and video. Yet their social pages can still become a sterile stream of: Products → Promotions → Events → Photos → “Contact us” → Buy now

The model

Build the capability. Keep the knowledge inside.

We don't take your content away from your team. We make your team better at creating it.

The agency model

Organisation → External agency → Content → Audience

The agency spends months learning your business, and the result is often generic copy, long approval cycles and no capability left behind.

The Story Funnel model

Organisation → Internal expertise → Training & strategy → Better content → Audience → Growth

The expertise already exists inside your organisation. We build the capability to communicate it directly, authentically and commercially.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Story Funnel?

Story Funnel helps organisations train their internal teams to turn their expertise into strategic content that attracts attention, builds trust and drives growth and sales.

Who is it for?

Organisations that already have knowledgeable people and internal teams, and want to communicate much better online. That includes corporates, SMEs, professional services, healthcare, education, hospitality, real estate, non-profits and consumer brands.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

An agency creates content for you. We build your team's ability to create it themselves, so the knowledge and the skills stay inside your organisation.

What does the social media audit cover?

We review your LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube presence and tell you why your followers are not converting to sales.

How long does a programme take?

It depends on the size of your team and what you want to achieve. Start with the audit or get in touch, and we'll shape a plan with you.

Do you create the content for us?

Our focus is training and strategy, so your people can create strong content themselves. Ongoing advisory support is available as your team grows.
